METHOD & DEFINITIONS

Understand what you are reading.

A guide to the fixed research workflow and the saved evaluation.

The six-role workflow

This dashboard reads saved research outputs. It does not run models, change prompts, or recalculate the paper’s scores.

The information timeline

  1. Information cutoff

    The date that bounds the saved information used for the assessment.

  2. Forecast target

    The next trading date assessed by the workflow.

  3. Observed outcome

    The saved anomaly label used for later evaluation.

Successful execution does not prove that all historical news was available at the time. Historical news availability remains a research limitation.

Scores and thresholds

Risk score
A model’s downside-tail risk intensity from 0 to 1. It is not a calibrated probability.
Saved threshold
The threshold applied on that date, using earlier resolved outcomes. The saved method is expanding past-only F1 selection.
Alert
The saved binary prediction made by comparing the role’s score with its threshold.

How to read the evaluation metrics

ROC AUC
How well scores rank anomaly dates above other dates across possible thresholds. A value of 0.5 is the chance ranking reference.
Average precision
A summary of precision and recall across score thresholds. It is useful when anomaly dates are rare.
F1 score
The harmonic mean of precision and recall for the saved threshold decisions.
MCC
Matthews correlation coefficient. It uses all four outcome counts and ranges from −1 to 1.
Precision
The share of alerts that were observed anomalies.
Recall
The share of observed anomalies that received an alert.

What is public?
