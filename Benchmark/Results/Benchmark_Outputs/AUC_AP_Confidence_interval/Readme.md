This folder contains the results of sequence preserving bootstrapping to compute confidence intervals of AUC ROC and AP for GARCH and Models with and without news. Specifically use files in this folder for anything related to confidence intervals of AUC ROC and AP. Output files for model with news are of form "{Asset}_Combined_ML_GARCH_BootstrapSummary_news.csv", and for GARCH and model without news is of form "{Asset}_Combined_ML_GARCH_BootstrapSummary.csv"
